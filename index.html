Privacy Policy for Stack Tower

Effective date: 5 November, 2026

Stack Tower is a mobile arcade game developed by HIS GRACE CORPORATE SERVICES.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Stack Tower application handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

Stack Tower is designed to operate offline and does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to the developer.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal details such as their name, email address, or phone number.

2. Internet Connection

The game's core functionality is designed to work without an internet connection. The app does not intentionally send gameplay or personal information to a remote server.

3. Advertising and Analytics

Stack Tower does not include advertising or third-party analytics services, provided that no such services are included in the published version of the app.

4. Local Data Storage

If the game stores scores, settings, or gameplay progress, that information may be saved locally on your device. This local information is not intentionally transmitted to the developer.

You can remove locally stored app data through your device's settings or by uninstalling the application. Uninstalling may remove saved progress.

5. Third-Party Services

Stack Tower is designed not to rely on third-party services that collect personal information. If third-party services are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain their relevant data practices.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not intentionally collect personal information from children or other users. The app's actual target audience and content rating are determined separately through the applicable Google Play Console declarations.

7. Data Security

Because Stack Tower is designed not to collect or transmit personal information, the developer does not maintain a server-side database of users' personal information through the game.

Users should protect their devices using the security features available on their operating systems.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

The developer does not intentionally retain personal information collected through Stack Tower. Any locally stored scores, settings, or progress may remain on the device until the user clears the app's data or uninstalls it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on the page hosting this policy, along with a revised effective date when appropriate.
